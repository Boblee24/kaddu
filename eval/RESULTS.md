## 3. Simulation: who gets paid

`pnpm simulate`: workers are randomly assigned clips (2 gold first, ~25% gold after) and run through
the real pipeline (signatures, checks, gold, judge, k=3 consensus, payout rules) on in-memory Postgres.
Honest workers type the truth with 0–2 typos and sometimes no tone marks; lazy workers paste the draft;
malicious workers send injections, gibberish, other clips' text, or real-looking word salad.

**Sats leaked** = sats paid for a transcript that is actually wrong (CER > 0.15 vs the hidden truth).
Sats to lazy workers can be legitimate when a synthetic draft happens to be right.

| Run | Honest sats | Lazy sats | Malicious sats | Sats leaked | Clips done | Disputed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 70/20/10, mock judge | 1890 | 0 | 0 | 0 (0%) | 15 | 1 |
| 70/20/10, no judge | 1830 | 0 | 0 | 0 (0%) | 15 | 1 |
| 40/30/30, mock judge | 750 | 0 | 0 | 0 (0%) | 8 | 8 |

Sats per finished clip (consensus payouts ÷ clips done, 70/20/10): **70 sats**.
Gold (qualification + hidden check) payouts in that run: 840 sats. With only 24 clips, gold
payouts dominate; on a real queue they shrink to ~25% of clips.
LLM cost: 85 judge API calls in §2 including retries (70 ok, failed: {"api_error":5,"timeout":10}); 2 submission(s) got no verdict after 4 tries and were scored without a judge. Token cost not metered by the app. Price table: 20 sats base, +10 low-confidence draft, +10 if > 6 s.

## Known limits
- 3 colluding keys that pass gold can agree on a wrong transcript. Mitigations: random assignment,
  ~25% hidden gold, sponsor spot checks, daily caps. Not simulated here.
- The live judge is an LLM: results can vary slightly between runs.
- Fraud cases are builder-written; real attackers will be more varied.
